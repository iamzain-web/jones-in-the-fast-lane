## Deliberate deviations

Everything the port does that Sierra did not. Each is a named switch at the site, so the
original can be restored and compared against. Nothing here is a bug fix or a tidy-up.

| Switch | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| `Player.StartingFoodWeeks` | off | Hands a new player food, which the original does not. |
| `TurnStart.SkipWeekOneHardship` | off | Skips spoilage, starvation and the doctor in week 1 only. |
| `MainViewModel.SmoothMotion` | **on** | **Presentation only.** Draws the in-between positions of the marble, the turn-start notice, Wild Willy, the ambulance and the win sequence's Jones, and redraws at ~60Hz instead of the original's 25ms step rate. Every step the scripts specify still happens, at the same moment, landing in the same place — the marble still takes one path index per tick and the notice still rests for `(proc0_3 240)`. Off restores the original's stepping exactly. Cel animation (the newspaper's fly-in, the stars and confetti) and the talker's mouth are deliberately NOT touched. The BOARD WALKER now is: with this on, `tools/smooth_walk.py` has generated three interpolated frames between each pair of its four cels, and `WalkerFrame` picks one by how far through the cel the wall clock is. The cel rate itself is the script's (`ticksToDo 10`) either way; the extra frames are made-up art, live only in `assets/png4x` as `*_sN.png`, and deleting them — or turning this off — leaves the original's four. |
| `Player.ShowPerGoalProgress` | **on** | **Presentation only.** Draws a percentage beside each of the four goal sliders on the read-only goals screen. The original prints no per-goal figure anywhere: `viewGoals` reports one number per player covering all four goals, and `select3` shows per-goal progress only as a marker's position. |
